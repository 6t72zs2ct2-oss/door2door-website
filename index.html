Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know.

Quick answers about our mobile service, booking, pricing, and what to expect.

Booking & Your Appointment

How do I book an appointment?

Choose your vehicle type, service, add-ons, date and time through our online booking page. Enter your contact and vehicle information, review your booking, and submit your request. We'll review and confirm your appointment.

Do I need to be home during the appointment?

Not necessarily. As long as we have safe access to the vehicle and the location, you may go about your day. We may need to contact you if we have questions about the vehicle or service.

How long does a detail take?

Service time depends on the package, vehicle size, and condition. Larger or heavily soiled vehicles may require additional time. We'll do our best to provide an estimated timeframe when confirming your appointment.

Can you come to my home, apartment, or workplace?

Yes. We can provide mobile detailing at homes, workplaces, and other approved locations as long as there is a safe and suitable place to work. For apartments, condos, HOAs, or commercial properties, please make sure mobile detailing is permitted at the location.

What happens if it rains or the weather is unsafe?

Weather conditions can affect certain services, especially exterior detailing and paint protection. If weather conditions make it unsafe or impractical to perform your service properly, we'll contact you to discuss rescheduling or an alternative solution.

Mobile Service

How does mobile detailing work?

We come to your home, workplace, or another approved location with our own professional equipment, water, power, and detailing products. You don't need to drive anywhere or wait at a shop—we bring the service to you.

Do I need to provide water?

No. Door2Door Detailing normally brings our own water and equipment. In the unlikely event of an equipment issue or emergency, we may ask for access to a water source as a temporary backup.

Do I need to provide electricity?

No. We normally bring our own power equipment. In the unlikely event of an equipment issue or emergency, we may ask for access to a standard electrical outlet as a temporary backup.

Is there an additional travel fee?

0–20 miles is free, 20–30 miles is +$20, 30–40 miles is +$30, and 40+ miles requires contacting us for availability and pricing.

Vehicle Preparation

What should I do before you arrive?

Please remove personal belongings and trash from your vehicle before your appointment. This allows us to focus on cleaning and detailing your vehicle properly. If significant trash or personal belongings need to be removed, a $15–$20 surcharge may apply.

Do I need to wash or vacuum my car first?

No. That's what you're hiring us for. Simply remove your personal belongings and trash, and make sure we have safe access to the vehicle.

Can there be additional charges?

Our listed prices cover vehicles in normal condition. Additional charges may apply for excessive dirt, heavy stains, severe pet hair, or conditions requiring significantly more time. We will communicate any applicable additional charge before performing the additional work.

Cleaning & Results

Can you remove pet hair and heavy stains?

Yes. Pet hair removal, seat shampoo, carpet shampoo, and spot treatments are available as add-on services. We use professional cleaning methods to achieve the best possible results. However, heavy, deep-set, or permanent stains may not be completely removable. Results depend on the type of stain, how long it has been present, and the condition and material of the surface. We cannot guarantee 100% stain removal, but we will always do our best to improve the appearance of the affected area.

Can you remove scratches from my paint?

Our 1-Step Paint Correction can improve the appearance of many light-to-moderate paint imperfections. Results depend on the depth and condition of the paint. Deeper scratches may not be completely removable. The standalone 1-Step Paint Correction package is exterior only and does not include interior detailing.
